🌟 2026 MLCommons Rising Star

I am a tenure-track Assistant Professor of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Texas Tech University, since Fall 2026. I received my Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from The University of Texas at Austin advised by Prof. Radu Marculescu.

My research is in Trustworthy and Efficient AI. I develop machine learning algorithms and architectures that do more with less, delivering strong performance with less data, less computation, and lower cost while staying reliable in high-stakes settings. My recent focus is on Foundation Models (Vision-Language Models, Multimodal Models, World Models, and Agentic AI), Digital Twins, and Quantum Machine Learning, and on making them practical for Biomedical Imaging and Healthcare, Computer Vision for Societal Impact, and Physical and Embodied AI. My long-term goal is to bring the benefits of AI to every hospital, device, and community that needs them. This work has appeared at CVPR, NeurIPS, MICCAI, ICCV, MIDL, WACV, and ICASSP.

I completed research internships at GE HealthCare, NIH, and Bosch AI, and received the NIH Summer IRTA Fellowship, the Texas Health Catalyst Award, and the Discovery to Impact Award.

📢 I am actively looking for self-motivated Ph.D. students starting Spring or Fall 2027. If you are interested, please read the Prospective Students section.

Prospective Students

I have Ph.D. openings for Spring and Fall 2027 in trustworthy and efficient AI, with projects in Physical and Embodied AI, Computer Vision, Biomedical Imaging and Healthcare, Trustworthy and Secure AI, Neuroimaging (Alzheimer's disease, dementia, and autism), and AI for Good.
